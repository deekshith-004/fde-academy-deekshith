Exercise 3 — Stakeholder Update Email for a Delayed Integration

Task 1 — Executive Version

Subject: Integration Completion Moved to Tuesday

Hi [Steering Committee],

The system integration will now be completed on Tuesday instead of Friday due to an unexpected compatibility issue identified during final validation.

The issue does not affect existing data or current operations.

Our team is implementing the required fix and completing validation.

The revised target is Tuesday, with no action required from the client team.

We will provide confirmation once the integration has completed successfully.

Rajesh

Task 2 — Technical Lead Version

Subject: Integration Timeline Update — Authentication Fix and Tuesday Completion

Hi [Technical Lead name],

The integration completion date has moved from Friday to Tuesday after we identified additional authentication complexity during testing.

The issue is related to compatibility between the authentication flow and the client's identity provider. We are updating the authentication handling, validating the revised flow, and then completing the remaining integration tests.

The updated plan is:

Complete the authentication fix.

Run the revised integration and validation tests.

Confirm end-to-end results before Tuesday's production completion.

No action is required from your side at this stage. We will share the updated test results once validation is complete.

Rajesh

Task 3 — Channel and RACI

Channel Choice

Steering Committee version: Email

Why: The schedule change affects a project commitment, so email provides a formal and traceable record.

Technical Lead version: Email

Why: The technical lead needs a clear record of the revised timeline, technical issue, fix approach, and testing plan.
