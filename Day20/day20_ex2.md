Reflections

IT Security may seem important because of its title, but its interest in the quality outcome is low, so Monitor is appropriate until security requirements are affected.

IT Security would move to Manage Closely if the deployment created a major network/security issue. Data Governance would do so if retention, ownership, classification, or compliance became a significant issue.

The Accountable owner does not always have to be the only person in Manage Closely, but the map should be checked if the two seem inconsistent.
